    Ogden ko gharma chha pipe bomb bhetiyepachhi Utah ka yuwakmathi ghrina aparadhko arop

    Sanghiya abhiyojakharu bhanchhan, Skyler AJ Rose, 22, le ek synagogue udaaune online dhamki die. Anusandhankartaharule unko Ogden gharbata chha karyashil pipe bomb, dui rifle ra bomb banaune bhag baramad gareko bataeka chhan.

    OPलेखक Orbit Postअक्टोबर 23, 2026 · 1:12 UTC3 मिनेट पढाइ
    साझा गर्नुहोस्
    यसमा पढ्नुहोस्ENESNEHIFR

    मुख्य बुँदाहरू

    • Salt Lake City ko ek sanghiya grand jury le Ogden ka 22 barshiya ek yuwaklai ghrina aparadh garne prayas lagayatka arop lagayeko chha.
    • Skyler AJ Rose lai October 21 ma abhiyog lagayeko Justice Department le bihibar, October 22 ma ghoshana garyo.
    • Adalat kagjatma ullekh arop anusar, ek social media prayogkartale December 23, 2025 ma dherai dhamki dine post gare.

    Salt Lake City ko ek sanghiya grand jury le Ogden ka 22 barshiya ek yuwaklai ghrina aparadh garne prayas lagayatka arop lagayeko chha. Anusandhankartaharule unko gharma chha wata improvised pipe bomb bheteko rar unle ek synagogue udaaune dhamki dine online post gareko bataeka chhan.

    Skyler AJ Rose lai October 21 ma abhiyog lagayeko Justice Department le bihibar, October 22 ma ghoshana garyo. Aropharuma darta nagariyeko vinashkari upkaran rakheko, manis marne wa ghaite banaune ra wisfotakle bhawan nashta garne dhamki, ra ghrina aparadh garne prayas lagayat chhan, Utah District ka lagi US Attorney ko karyalayale bhaneko chha. Rose le shukrabar, October 23 diuso 1:30 baje Salt Lake City ko Orrin G. Hatch US Courthouse ko courtroom 8.4 ma sanghiya adalatma pahilo patak upasthit hune talika chha. unle sanghiya muddama ahilesamma nivedan gareka chhainan.

    विज्ञापन

    December ma post gariyeka dhamkiharu

    Adalat kagjatma ullekh arop anusar, ek social media prayogkartale December 23, 2025 ma dherai dhamki dine post gare. Ek postma bhanieko thiyo, "I wanna blow up a synagogue." Anusandhankartaharule tee post Rose basne gharko internet theganabata gareko patta lagae. FBI le Ogden City Police Department lai ghar jana sahayogka lagi samparka garyo, ra adhikariharule social media khata Rose kai bhaeko pushti gare, Justice Department le bhaneko chha.

    Dhamkiharuko anusandhan hundai garda Rose lai gata barsa sthaniya adhikariharule pakrau gareka thie, Washington Examiner le janayeko chha. December 2025 ma pahile gharma paseka sthaniya adhikariharule unko sutne kothama pipe bomb jasto dekheko bataeka thie, tyas belaka sthaniya report anusar, ra sanghiya anusandhan jari raheko bela unle rajya aropharu samana gare.

    Chha pipe bomb ra dui rifle

    Search warrant antargat gariyeko khojima, kanun karyanwayanle chha wata improvised wisfotak pipe bomb ra ek nishkriya upkaran baramad garyo, Justice Department le bhaneko chha. Adhikariharule thap pipe bomb banauna prayog huna sakne wisfotak purwasamagri ra bhagharu, ek kalo Samsung cellphone, ra magazine ra goligaththa sahit dui rifle pani bhete. Vibhagle pipe bombharu karyashil bhaeko varnan garyo.

    Pachhi phone ko khojile November 2025 ma Rose le afu najikko synagogue online khojeko dekhaeko adalat kagjatle bhancha. Phone ma kathit rupma bal yaun durvyavahar samagrika praman, sayaun cache garieka tasbir ra screenshot sahit, pani bhaeko arop chha.

    Abhiyojakharule kunai nishchit synagogue lai laksya bhaneka chhainan. Ogden police le gata barsa kunai nishchit synagogue laksit bhaeko praman nabhaeko, ra dhamkiharu Yahudi samudaya ra Yahudi pujasthalharutira nirdeshit bhaeko bataeko thiyo.

    Adhikariharu bhanchhan, dhamki samayma nai rokio

    "Aajko abhiyogle ek synagogue mathi hinsatmak akraman garne kathit yojanalai tyahi rokchha", Justice Department Civil Rights Division ki Assistant Attorney General Harmeet K. Dhillon le bhanin. "Justice Department le ameriki upasakharulai hinsatmak dhamkibata jogauna, jahai bhae pani, nirnayak kadam chalnechha."

    Utah District ki US Attorney Melissa Holyoak le pujasthal surakshako sharan hunu parcha, darr hoina bhanin, ra kohi ghaite hunu aghi nai kathit dhamki patta lagaekoma FBI ra sthaniya police sajhedarharulai dhanyavad diin. FBI Salt Lake City field office ka bishesh agent prabhari Robert Bohls le FBI le harek dhamkilai gambhir rupma line batae. Ogden Police Chief Jake Sube le yati thulo dhamkika lagi kunai ek agency sanga sabai jankari nahune batae, ra muddalai sanghiya, rajya ra sthaniya sajhedarharule jankari sajha gareko udaharan bhane.

    FBI Salt Lake City field office ra Ogden City Police Department le sangai muddako anusandhan gare, Utah Department of Public Safety, Davis County ra Weber County bomb squad, Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, ra Weber County Attorney ko karyalayako sahayogma. Special Assistant US Attorney John Auchter le mudda chalairaheka chhan.

    Abhiyog bhaneko arop matra ho. Rose adalatma uchit shankabhanda mathi doshi pramanit nabhaesamma nirdosh maninchhan.
