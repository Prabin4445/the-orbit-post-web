    Najran ma Saudi AH-6i helicopter durghatana, dui chalak ko mrityu

    Saudi netritwa ko gathabandhan le aitabar bhaneko chha ki National Guard ko AH-6i jasusi helicopter prawidhik kharabi ka karan Najran khetra ma khasyo ra teska dui chalak ko mrityu bhayo.

    OPलेखक Orbit Postअक्टोबर 18, 2026 · 4:50 UTC2 मिनेट पढाइ
    साझा गर्नुहोस्
    यसमा पढ्नुहोस्ENESNEHIFR

    मुख्य बुँदाहरू

    • RIYADH.
    • Gathabandhan ka prawakta Brigadier General Turki al-Maliki le helicopter prawidhik kharabi ka karan khaseko batae.
    • Gathabandhan le dui chalak ko naam dieko chhaina.

    RIYADH. Saudi AH-6i jasusi helicopter dakshin paschimi Saudi Arab ko Najran khetra ma durghatanagrasta bhayo ra teska duwai chalak ko mrityu bhayo, Saudi netritwa ko gathabandhan le aitabar ek bayan ma bhaneko chha.

    Gathabandhan ka prawakta Brigadier General Turki al-Maliki le helicopter prawidhik kharabi ka karan khaseko batae. Biman National Guard Mantralaya ko thiyo ra tyo mantralaya ko khetra bhitra khaseko bayan ma bhanieko chha. Jahaj ma sawar dui jana teska chalak thiye, ra anya Saudi report haru le uniharu lai dui adhikrit bhaneka chhan.

    विज्ञापन

    Bayan le ke bhanena

    Gathabandhan le dui chalak ko naam dieko chhaina. Yemen sanga ko sima ma parne thulo khetra Najran bhitra durghatana ko thyakkai thau dieko chhaina, ra durghatana ko thyakkai samay pani dieko chhaina. Kun prawidhik kharabi le helicopter khasayo bhanne bare pani kunai vivaran dieko chhaina, ra janch ko kunai nishkarsha ghoshana gareko chhaina.

    AH-6i bhaneko Little Bird haluka helicopter ko sashastra jasusi sanskaran ho. Saudi National Guard le yo model nigarani ra haluka akraman kaam ma chalaunchha. Aitabar ko bayan durghatana bare pahilo adhikarik shabd thiyo.

    X ma chhuttai dabi

    Durghatana ko ghoshana unline ma chaliraheko thulo bahas ko bich ma ayo. Shanibar, Houthi sainya prawakta Yahya Saree le X ma ek post ma bhane ki Houthi bal le Najran najik ek Saudi Apache helicopter khasale. Apache le sima khetra ma Houthi sthan haru tir shatru gatividhi gariraheko unle batae.

    Duwai bayan bare report garne Reuters le bhanyo, duwai paksha eutai helicopter bare boliraheka thiye ki thienan bhanne spashta chhaina. Ullekha garieka model haru farak biman hun. Apache bhari akraman helicopter ho, ra gathabandhan le naam dieko AH-6i dherai sano haluka jasusi biman ho. Gathabandhan ko bayan le Najran durghatana ko karan prawidhik kharabi bataeko chha ra Houthi dabi ko ullekha gareko chhaina.

    Sima ma tanaab ko hapta

    Najran Saudi Arab ko dakshin paschimi sima ma chha, uttari Yemen ma Houthi haru le kabja gareko khetra ko samu. Saudi netritwa ko gathabandhan le barshaun dekhi Yemen sarkar lai Houthi biruddha samarthan gareko chha, ra sima khetra haru shanta samay ma pani tanaab ma rahanchhan.

    Yo hapta shanta thiyena. Kehi din aghi Saudi bimansthal haru ma Houthi akraman ko report ayo, jasma gat hapta Riyadh ko King Khalid Antarastriya Bimansthal ma bhaeko ghatak akraman ma kamtima 13 jana mareka thiye. Ti akraman pachhi, America le afna sarkari karmachari haru lai bishesh anumati bina Saudi bimansthal prayog garna rok lagayo.

    Ahile lai, pushti bhaeka tathya thorai chhan. National Guard ko AH-6i Najran ma khasyo, adhikarik karan prawidhik kharabi ho, ra dui chalak mareka chhan. Tes bhanda badhi kehi, Houthi dabi eutai biman bare ho ki hoina sahit, kunai paksha bata pushti bhaeko chhaina.

    Srot haru

    Gathabandhan ko bayan bare Cairo bata Reuters ko report, uhi bayan bare Arab News ko coverage, ra Sky News Arabia ko coverage.
