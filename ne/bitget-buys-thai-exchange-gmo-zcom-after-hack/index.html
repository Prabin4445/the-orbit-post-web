    Bitget le $387.5 million hack ko hapta pachhi Thai exchange kinchha

    Bitget le BTGT Management marfat Z.com EX pachhadi ko ijajat prapta Thai exchange kinne yojana banaeko chha, akramankari haru le usko wallet bata karib $387.5 million nikale ko hapta pachhi.

    OPलेखक Orbit Postअक्टोबर 20, 2026 · 16:45 UTC3 मिनेट पढाइ
    साझा गर्नुहोस्
    यसमा पढ्नुहोस्ENESNEHIFR

    मुख्य बुँदाहरू

    • Bitget le mangalbar, October 20, 2026 ma GMO-Z.com Cryptonomics kinne yojana banaeko bhanyo, Z.com EX platform pachhadi ko ijajat prapta Thai crypto exchange.
    • Samjhauta le Bitget lai niyamit bazar ma chito marg dinchha.
    • Japan sthit GMO Internet le pahile September 24, 2026 ma prastawit share hastantaran khulasa garyo.

    Bitget le mangalbar, October 20, 2026 ma GMO-Z.com Cryptonomics kinne yojana banaeko bhanyo, Z.com EX platform pachhadi ko ijajat prapta Thai crypto exchange. Kretā BTGT Management (Thailand) ho, yasto company jas ma Bitget ko hit chha tara usle akar khulae na. Kharid mulya khulaiena.

    Samjhauta le Bitget lai niyamit bazar ma chito marg dinchha. Z.com EX February 2021 ma Thai SEC ra Artha Mantralaya ko ijajat sahit suru bhayo. Ijajat prapta exchange kinnu naya banaunu ra suru bata avedan dinu bhanda chito chha, ra yo kharid ko mukhya tarka tyahi ho.

    विज्ञापन

    Kinieko ijajat, banaiyeko hoina

    Japan sthit GMO Internet le pahile September 24, 2026 ma prastawit share hastantaran khulasa garyo. Paksha haru le Thai niyamak anumodan ra anya sharta ko adhin ma October 31, 2026 ma pura garne lakshya rakheka chhan.

    Bitget CEO Gracy Chen le Thailand dakshin purva Asia ko digital sampatti bikas ko mahatwapurna bhag ho ra samjhauta le Bitget lai tyahan sthaniya sanchalan sthapana garne mauka dinchha bhane. Thailand ma sthaniya upasthiti mahatwapurna chha, jahan ijajat, sthaniya niyam ra niyamak bharosa le ko thulo star ma sanchalan garna sakchha bhanne nirnaya garchha.

    Katha badalne hack

    Samaya nai katha ho. September 24, 2026 ma, GMO le samjhauta khulasa gare kai din, Bitget le akramankari haru le usko tato ra nyano wallet khali gariraheko patta lagayo. Noksan anuman $351.6 million bata badhera karib $387.5 million pugyo.

    TRM Labs le yasalai 2026 ma ekal crypto firm bata sabaibhanda thulo chori bhanyo. Bitget le September 28, 2026 ma bitcoin nikalne punah suru garyo ra grahak balance prabhawit bhaena bhanyo. Tee tathya le noksan metdaina. Tiniharu le niyamak ra grahak le kretā lai kasari mulyankan garnechhan bhanne frame dinchhan.

    Record rakam bharkharai gumaeko exchange le ijajat ko roop ma bharosa kinna khojiraheko chha. Bangkok ka niyamak haru le tyo paune ki paune chhaina nirnaya garnechhan. Tiniharu ko samiksha le tyati thulo ullanghan pachhi ijajat prapta platform chalaune suraksha, niyantran ra yogyata parikshan garnechha.

    Thailand le niyamit dhoka kholchha

    Thailand ko sandarbha le Bitget ahile kina bhitra jana chahanchha bhanne bujhna maddat garchha. October 16, 2026 dekhi lagu bhaeka naya niyam haru le yogya bitcoin ra ether ETF utpadan haru lai Stock Exchange of Thailand ma suchikrit garna dinchha, SEC le pratyek utpadan anumodan garne gari.

    Tyo pariwartan le ijajat bina ko karobar hoina, niyamit channel marfat khulne bazar ko sanket dinchha. Ijajat prapta sthaniya exchange tyo khulapan ko najik chha, Thai aadhar nabhaeko bideshi platform bhanda.

    Aba ke hunchha

    Pura hunu anumodan ma bhar parchha. Yadi Thai niyamak haru le October 31, 2026 samma hastantaran anumodan gare, Bitget le ijajat prapta Thai sanchalan ra sthaniya toli paunchha. Yadi tiniharu le dhilai gare wa kada sharta rakhe, samaya sarnechha ra bharosa ko lagat badhnechha.

    Grahak haru le hirasat, suraksha ra sthaniya nigarani bare spashta kathan khojnechhan. Pratispardhi haru le hack pachhi ko adhigrahan dakshin purva Asia ma prawesh ko model banchha ki ijajat bharosa punah nirman bhanda chito kinna sakinna bhanne chetawani banchha hernechhan.
