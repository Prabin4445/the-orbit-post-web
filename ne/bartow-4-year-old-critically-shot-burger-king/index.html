    बार्टो, फ्लोरिडा: Burger King मा कारभित्र बुबाको बन्दुकले आफैलाई गोली हानेपछि ३ वर्षीय बालकको मृत्यु

    ९:३० बजेको ब्रीफिङमा शेरिफ Grady Judd ले भने अनुसार, बिहीबार साँझ 109 E Van Fleet Drive को Burger King मा गाडीभित्र बुबाको Glock ले अनुहारमा आफैलाई गोली हानेपछि चौथो जन्मदिन आउन चार दिन बाँकी रहेका ३ वर्षीय बालकको Lakeland Regional Health मा मृत्यु भयो।

    OPलेखक Orbit Postअक्टोबर 23, 2026 · 3:14 UTC · अद्यावधिक अक्टोबर 23, 2026 3:38 UTC3 मिनेट पढाइ

    Updated 03:38 UTC: Bartow ko Burger King ma bihibar sanjh gadi bhitra afna bubako handgun le afailai goli hanera 3 barshiya balak ko mrityu bhaeko chha, Sheriff Grady Judd le 9:30 baje ko briefing ma patrakarharulai bhane, 4 barshiya gambhir ghaite bhanne pahila reportharu sachyaudai.

    Judd ka anusar goli karib 6:30 baje chale ko thiyo. Char mahina aghi Connecticut bata Florida sareko pariwarle 109 E Van Fleet Drive ko Burger King ko drive thru bata khana kinna khojda bubako credit card aswikar bhayo. Bubale card ko samasya milauna gadi parking ma roke. Cardharu herna niurida unle afno Glock handgun kamar bata jhikera gadi bhitra rakhe, Judd le bhane.

    विज्ञापन

    Pachhadi ko seat ma bina rok kheliraheka, chautho janmadin char din baki raheka balakle banduk uthae ra anuharma afailai goli hane, Judd le bhane. Anusandhankartaharule gadiko chhat ma, karib bich ma, goliko pwal bhete. Bubale thulo awaj sune, bahira niskie ra attiera gadi waripari daude, ra chheuko gadiki mahilale 911 ma phone garin, Judd le parking ko surveillance video varnan gardai bhane.

    Balaklai Lakeland Regional Health lagiyo, jaha karib 7:30 baje unlai mrit ghoshana gariyo. Judd ka anusar duwai amabuba Lowe's ka manager hun. Bihibar sanjh kohi pakrau parean. Jasusharule anusandhan ko jankari State Attorney ko karyalayama pathaunchhan, jasle arop lagne ki nalagne nirnaya garnechha. Bartow police le anusandhan ko netritwa gariraheko chha, Polk County Sheriff ko karyalaya ko sahayog ma.

    Florida ko Bartow ma ek Burger King ko parking sthanma bihibar sanjh gadi bhitra goli lagera 4 barshiya baccha gambhir rupma ghaite bhaeka chhan, Polk County Sheriff ko karyalayale bhaneko chha.

    Goli Bartow ko 109 E Van Fleet Drive ma raheko Burger King ma lageko ho. Bacchalai gadi bhitrai goli lagyo ra unko chot gambhir chha, sheriff ko karyalayale sthaniya madhyamharulai bhaneko chha. Bacchako naam sarwajanik garieko chhaina.

    Kasle anusandhan gardaicha

    Bartow Police Department le anusandhanko netritwa gariraheko chha, ra Polk County Sheriff ko karyalayale sahayog gariraheko chha, dubai agencyle bhane. Sheriff ko karyalayako X page ma post gariyeko ghatanasthalko tasbirle pachhadi medical kit jasto dekhine seto Toyota sedan dekhaeko The Ledger le janayeko chha.

    Polk County Sheriff Grady Judd le bihibar rati 9:30 baje thap vivaran dine apeksha garieko thiyo. Yo khabar prakashit hunda samma sthaniya madhyamharule tyo briefing bata naya tathya report gareka thienan, ra sheriff ko karyalayako adharbhut vivaran pariwartan bhaeko thiena: ek sano baccha, gadi bhitra lageko goli, ra Bartow ma restaurant ko parking.

    Adhikariharule ke bhanean

    Anusandhankartaharule banduk kasari chalyo bhanera pushti gareka chhainan. Uniharule banduk kasle chalairaheko thiyo, goli durghatanale lagyo wa janajani, wa ghatana hunda gadi bhitra aru kohi thie ki bhanera bhane chhainan.

    Adhikariharule sandigdha, pakrau wa sambhabit aropharubare kunai vivaran dieka chhainan, FOX 13 le janayeko chha. Uniharule bacchalai upcharka lagi kaha lagyio bhanena, ra bihibar sanjh sheriff ko karyalayaka pahila reportharuma chot gambhir bhanieko bahek bacchako awasthabare update dieka chhainan.

    Yasto muddaka pahila ghantaharuma yasto maunta samanya ho, jab jasusharu ajhai ghatanasthalma bhaeka manisharulai sodhpuchh gardaichan ra gadi bhitraka ghatanaharuko kram milairaheka chhan. Tara yesko artha Bartow ko kendriya prashna aja rati pani anuttarit chha: fast food restaurant ma rokieko gadi bhitra 4 barshiya baccha kasari goli lagyo, ra banduk kasle samateko thiyo.

    Aba ke hunchha

    Bartow police ra sheriff ka anusandhankartaharule banduk kasako thiyo, gadi bhitra kasle yasalai pugna sakthyo, ra goli chalnu aghika kshanharuma ke bhayo bhanera sthapit garnu parnechha. Tee javabharule yo mudda aparadhik abhiyogma pugcha, wa bacchako pug bhitra chhodiyeko banduk chalera bacchale mulya chukaeko nishkarshama, bhanera nirnaya garnechan.

    Ahileka lagi, pushti bhaeka tathyaharu thorai ra spashta chhan. Bihibar sanjh, October 22 ma 109 E Van Fleet Drive ma gadi bhitra 4 barshiya baccha goli lage. Chot gambhir varnan gariyo. Bartow police ko netritwama ra Polk County Sheriff ko karyalayako sahayogma dui agencyle anusandhan gardaichan. Sheriff Judd le 9:30 baje bolne talika thiyo.

    Goli kanda bare jankari bhaeka kasailai anusandhan chalaune agency Bartow police wa Polk County Sheriff ko karyalayalai samparka garnu parcha. Anusandhankartaharule golikandako paristhiti wa bacchako awasthabare update die pachhi yo khabar adyavadik garinechha.
