    Cole Palmer Beats IShowSpeed 18-0 at EA FC 27, and the Clip Is Everywhere

    Chelsea midfielder Cole Palmer beat streamer IShowSpeed 18-0 on EA Sports FC 27 on a livestream, with no shots allowed for his opponent.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 16, 2026 · 13:28 UTC4 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • Chelsea midfielder Cole Palmer beat streamer IShowSpeed 18-0 in a match on EA Sports FC 27 played on a livestream.
    • A clip shared by the 433 account on Instagram spread widely after the match.
    • This was not a close game that got away late.

    Chelsea midfielder Cole Palmer beat streamer IShowSpeed 18-0 in a match on EA Sports FC 27 played on a livestream. Palmer, 24, scored 18 unanswered goals. IShowSpeed, whose real name is Darren Watkins Jr, did not register a single shot. The clip is now everywhere.

    A clip shared by the 433 account on Instagram spread widely after the match. In it, Speed reacts with disbelief as the score grows. Palmer stays calm and points him to the final statistics at the end. Sportskeeda reported the match and the viral reaction. The Sun also reported the 18-0 score and the zero shots line.

    Advertisement

    This was not a close game that got away late. It was a shutout from start to finish, in a football video game, by a professional footballer playing on the home turf of one of the biggest streamers in the world.

    Why the clip took off

    The appeal is simple. Palmer is a top Premier League player. Speed is one of the most watched streamers in the world, and his audience knows EA FC well. Fans expect the streamer to hold his own in a video game, even against a pro. Instead, the pro controlled the match so fully that his opponent never got a shot away.

    That gap is the joke and the story at once. Speed built his livestream energy on loud reactions and big confidence. Palmer barely changed expression while the goals kept coming. The contrast gave the clip its share value. One side could not believe the score. The other side treated it like a normal result and showed the stats to prove it.

    Speed has played footballers before

    This was not the first time Speed has played EA FC against a footballer. He has played before against Alejandro Balde, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Hugo Ekitike. Those matches gave his audience a familiar format: the streamer tests himself against a real player in the game both sides know.

    The Palmer match stood apart because of the margin. An 18-0 win leaves no room for debate about luck, connection, or one bad half. Palmer scored again and again, and kept a clean sheet in game terms by allowing no shots. It is the kind of result that fans clip, repost, and send to friends who have not seen it yet.

    What the numbers say

    The final statistics told the story in one screen. Palmer had 18 goals. Speed had zero goals and zero shots. In a game built on creating chances, a zero shots line is rare at any level of casual play. It means the losing player never built an attack that ended in an attempt, let alone a goal.

    For Palmer, the match also fits his public image. He plays real football with calm control and clear finishing. In the clip, that same calm shows up with a controller in his hands. He did not need to perform for the stream. The score did the work.

    Back to real football

    The Sun reported Palmer is expected back on a real pitch when Chelsea face Everton on Saturday. That return gives the viral moment a short shelf life in the football calendar. By the weekend, Chelsea fans will judge him on the real game again.

    Still, the clip will travel further than most training updates or press lines. That crossover is why a video game result made sports pages.

    Key numbers

    • Final score: Cole Palmer 18, IShowSpeed 0
    • Shots for IShowSpeed: Zero
    • Goals: 18 unanswered goals for Palmer
    • Game: EA Sports FC 27, played on a livestream
    • Palmer age: 24
    • IShowSpeed real name: Darren Watkins Jr
    • Shared by: 433 account on Instagram, per reports of the viral clip
    • Next real match noted in reports: Chelsea face Everton on Saturday

    Why people keep sharing it

    Fans love a clear score, a famous name, and a reaction they can understand in five seconds. This clip has all three. A Premier League star wins 18-0. A famous streamer cannot get a shot. The winner points to the stats with no fuss. No long setup is needed, and no football knowledge is required to get why it is funny and striking at the same time.

    That is the whole event, and it is enough. Palmer won the game, Speed gave the reaction, and the internet did the rest.
