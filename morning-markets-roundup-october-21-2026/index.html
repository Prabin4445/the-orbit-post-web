    Bitcoin Tops $83,500 as Stocks Rise Before Tesla and Meta Results

    Bitcoin hit a one week high near $83,500, Asian stocks jumped, and US futures rose as investors waited for Tesla and Meta earnings after the close.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 21, 2026 · 14:30 UTC4 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • Bitcoin rose to about $83,500 on Wednesday, October 21, its highest level in a week, as Asian tech stocks climbed and investors waited for earnings from Tesla and Meta after the US close.
    • This roundup pulls the overnight tape together in one place.
    • Ether traded near $2,460 to $2,474, up about 0.6 to 0.8 percent across snapshots.

    Bitcoin rose to about $83,500 on Wednesday, October 21, its highest level in a week, as Asian tech stocks climbed and investors waited for earnings from Tesla and Meta after the US close. CoinDesk put the gain at about 0.6 percent since midnight UTC. A separate Finnhub snapshot showed bitcoin at $83,897, up 1.1 percent on the day. The two figures are snapshots taken at different times, and both point the same way. Crypto started the day firmer, not weaker.

    This roundup pulls the overnight tape together in one place. This site covers the SpaceX and Tesla merger committee story and the US futures move in separate articles. Here the point is how crypto, stocks, oil and interest rates fit together before a big earnings night.

    Advertisement

    Crypto overnight

    Ether traded near $2,460 to $2,474, up about 0.6 to 0.8 percent across snapshots. Solana was mixed by timing. The Finnhub snapshot showed Solana near $106.71, down 0.6 percent, while CoinDesk put Solana up 1 to 2 percent earlier in the Asian session. Traders should read that as a coin that rose early and then eased, not as two rival facts.

    Zcash gave the sharpest move. It jumped about 11 percent after developers set a November activation date for its NU7 upgrade. The upgrade cuts block times from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. Near Protocol led the CoinDesk 100 index with a 6 percent gain. Those moves beat the majors, but they rest on thinner trading. A big percentage gain in a smaller coin can reverse fast.

    Stocks and futures

    South Korea led Asian stocks. The Kospi rose 3.29 percent in the CoinDesk report, and another morning report put the gain at 3.7 percent. US futures pointed higher. CoinDesk put Nasdaq 100 futures up 0.44 percent. This site's own futures report put Nasdaq futures up 0.71 to 0.78 percent, S and P 500 futures up 0.24 to 0.31 percent, and Dow futures little changed. Again, the reports ran at different times in a moving premarket.

    Tuesday's US close gave the rally a base. The Dow rose 529.85 points, or 1.02 percent, to 52,495.88. The S and P 500 rose 0.28 percent to 7,712.57. Brent crude held above $110 a barrel, a level that keeps fuel and shipping costs in focus for companies reporting this week.

    The names moving

    SpaceX and Tesla have formed special committees to explore a merger, a story covered in full elsewhere on this site. The two companies together hold more than 30,000 bitcoin worth about $2.5 billion, which is one reason crypto traders watched the news closely. SpaceX shares rose about 8 percent. Super Micro jumped in premarket trading after strong preliminary numbers. Netflix fell about 6 percent on soft guidance. That split tells the story of this earnings season so far. Good numbers help, but weak guidance gets punished hard and fast.

    Tonight is the earnings test

    Tesla and Meta report after the US close today. Meta is the AI spending watch item. Investors want to know how much the company plans to spend on data centers and chips, and whether ad revenue still covers that bill. Tesla faces the usual questions on deliveries, margins and demand, now with the merger committee news in the background. Results from both companies will set the tone for tech stocks on Thursday, because index funds hold both names in size.

    Rates sit behind everything. The CME FedWatch tool showed an 85 percent chance the Federal Reserve holds rates steady next week, at its October 27 to 28 meeting. A hold is widely expected, so the words around the decision may move markets more than the decision itself.

    Key numbers

    Bitcoin: about $83,500, with a Finnhub snapshot at $83,897. Ether: near $2,460 to $2,474. Solana: near $106.71 in the Finnhub snapshot. Zcash: up about 11 percent. Near Protocol: up 6 percent. Kospi: up 3.29 percent in one report, 3.7 percent in another. Nasdaq futures: up 0.44 percent in one report, 0.71 to 0.78 percent in this site's report. Brent crude: above $110 a barrel. Dow close Tuesday: 52,495.88, up 529.85 points. S and P 500 close Tuesday: 7,712.57, up 0.28 percent. Fed hold odds: 85 percent.

    What this means for ordinary money

    Most workers meet this market through a 401k, not a trading screen. Tesla and Meta sit inside the index funds many retirement plans hold, so tonight's results can move a 401k balance even for people who own neither stock directly. A one day move is not a reason to change a long term plan. It is a reason to know what you own.

    Mortgage rates connect to bond yields, and yields connect to the Fed. With traders putting 85 percent odds on a hold next week, borrowers should not expect a sudden drop in mortgage rates from that meeting alone. Guidance matters for households too. When firms like Netflix guide soft and lose 6 percent in a session, pension and index funds feel it the same day. This season, what companies say about the next quarter counts more than what they earned in the last one. That is the test Tesla and Meta face tonight.
