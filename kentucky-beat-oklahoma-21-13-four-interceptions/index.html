    Kentucky Beat Oklahoma 21-13 as Sooners Throw Four Interceptions

    Jordan Castell returned an interception 41 yards for a touchdown in the final two minutes as No. 15 Kentucky scored 14 points in the fourth quarter to win 21 to 13 in Norman. Oklahoma outgained Kentucky but threw four interceptions and none by the Wildcats.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 18, 2026 · 3:20 UTC3 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • No. 15 Kentucky beat Oklahoma 21 to 13 at Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium in Norman on Saturday, October 17, 2026.
    • Oklahoma outgained Kentucky 346 yards to 230 and led for most of the night.
    • Kentucky opened the game with a touchdown drive, the first opening drive touchdown scored against Brent Venables and his Oklahoma defense this season.

    No. 15 Kentucky beat Oklahoma 21 to 13 at Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium in Norman on Saturday, October 17, 2026. The Wildcats scored 14 points in the fourth quarter and closed the game with two interceptions in the final two minutes. Jordan Castell returned an interception 41 yards for the touchdown that put Kentucky ahead for good, and Ty Bryant picked off John Mateer again with 27 seconds left to end it.

    Oklahoma outgained Kentucky 346 yards to 230 and led for most of the night. It did not matter. The Sooners threw four interceptions and Kentucky threw none, and that was the whole game. Mateer was intercepted three times in the final 15 minutes and 34 seconds, and he was sacked five times behind a beat up offensive line.

    Advertisement

    Kentucky strikes first, then hangs on

    Kentucky opened the game with a touchdown drive, the first opening drive touchdown scored against Brent Venables and his Oklahoma defense this season. Kenny Minchey found Willie Rodriguez in the end zone for a 7 to 0 lead. Oklahoma answered with a Tate Sandell field goal, then gambled deep in Kentucky territory in the second quarter. On fourth and 2, the Sooners tried a fake field goal pass, and Castell intercepted it. It was the first of four Kentucky takeaways through the air.

    Mateer steadied Oklahoma before halftime. He threw a 20 yard touchdown pass to Rocky Beers, and the Sooners led 10 to 7 at the break. Sandell added a second field goal in the third quarter, and Oklahoma led 13 to 7 with one quarter to play. Minchey finished 21 of 28 for 158 yards and two touchdowns, with no turnovers. Mateer finished 24 of 40 for 240 yards, one touchdown and three interceptions, and the fake field goal pick made four Oklahoma interceptions in all.

    The fourth quarter flips

    Kentucky took the lead early in the fourth quarter. A deflected Mateer pass was intercepted, and the Wildcats turned the short field into Minchey's second touchdown pass of the night and a 14 to 13 lead. Oklahoma got the ball back and drove again, needing about 40 more yards to reach field goal range with under two minutes left.

    The pressure ended it. After his fifth sack of the day, Mateer forced a throw, Castell stepped in front of it for his second interception, and he ran 41 yards for a touchdown and a 21 to 13 lead. Oklahoma had one last drive, and Bryant intercepted Mateer near midfield with 27 seconds left. Kentucky knelt out its fifth straight win.

    What it means

    Kentucky is 6 to 1 and 4 to 1 in the SEC, bowl eligible in October, and tied near the top of the conference standings. The Wildcats are 3 to 0 on the road in SEC play, and the defense won this one on a night the offense managed only 230 yards.

    Oklahoma falls to 3 to 3 and 1 to 2 in the SEC, one week after beating Texas. The Sooners Wire wrote that the College Football Playoff picture is now out the window and Oklahoma is playing for bowl eligibility the rest of the season. The Sooners go to Mississippi State next week. Kentucky hosts Vanderbilt on October 24.
