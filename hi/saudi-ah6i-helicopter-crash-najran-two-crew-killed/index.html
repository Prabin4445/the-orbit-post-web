    Najran mein Saudi AH-6i helicopter durghatnagrat, dono chalak dal ke sadasya mare

    Saudi netritva wale gathbandhan ne ravivar ko kaha ki National Guard ka ek AH-6i jasusi helicopter takneeki kharabi ke karan Najran khetra mein gir gaya aur uske dono chalak dal ke sadasya mare gaye.

    OPलेखक Orbit Postअक्टूबर 18, 2026 · 4:50 UTC3 मिनट में पढ़ें
    शेयर करें
    इसमें पढ़ेंENESNEHIFR

    मुख्य बिंदु

    • RIYADH.
    • Gathbandhan ke pravakta Brigadier General Turki al-Maliki ne kaha ki helicopter takneeki kharabi ke karan gira.
    • Gathbandhan ne dono chalak dal ke sadasyon ke naam nahi diye.

    RIYADH. Ek Saudi AH-6i jasusi helicopter dakshin paschimi Saudi Arab ke Najran khetra mein durghatnagrat ho gaya aur uske dono chalak dal ke sadasyon ki maut ho gayi, Saudi netritva wale gathbandhan ne ravivar ko ek bayan mein kaha.

    Gathbandhan ke pravakta Brigadier General Turki al-Maliki ne kaha ki helicopter takneeki kharabi ke karan gira. Viman National Guard Mantralaya ka tha aur usi mantralaya ke khetra mein gira, bayan mein kaha gaya. Jahaj par sawar dono log uske chalak dal the, aur anya Saudi reporton ne unhe do adhikari bataya.

    विज्ञापन

    Bayan mein kya nahi kaha gaya

    Gathbandhan ne dono chalak dal ke sadasyon ke naam nahi diye. Yemen ke saath seema par sthit bade khetra Najran ke andar durghatna ka sateek sthan nahi diya gaya, aur durghatna ka sateek samay bhi nahi diya gaya. Kis takneeki kharabi se helicopter gira, iska koi vivaran bhi nahi diya gaya, aur jaanch ke koi natije ghoshit nahi kiye gaye.

    AH-6i, Little Bird halke helicopter ka sashastra jasusi sanskaran hai. Saudi National Guard is model ko nigrani aur halke hamle ke kaam mein chalata hai. Ravivar ka bayan durghatna par pehla adhikarik shabd tha.

    X par ek alag dava

    Durghatna ki ghoshna online chal rahi tez behes ke beech aayi. Shanivar ko Houthi sainya pravakta Yahya Saree ne X par ek post mein kaha ki Houthi balon ne Najran ke paas ek Saudi Apache helicopter maar giraya. Unhone kaha ki Apache seema ke saath Houthi thikanon ke khilaf shatru gatividhi kar raha tha.

    Dono bayanon par report karne wale Reuters ne kaha ki yeh spasht nahi tha ki dono paksh ek hi helicopter ki baat kar rahe the ya nahi. Naam liye gaye model alag viman hain. Apache ek bhari hamla helicopter hai, aur gathbandhan dwara naam diya gaya AH-6i usse kahin chhota halka jasusi viman hai. Gathbandhan ke bayan ne Najran durghatna ka karan takneeki kharabi bataya aur Houthi dave ka ullekh nahi kiya.

    Seema par tanav ka hafta

    Najran Saudi Arab ki dakshin paschimi seema par hai, uttari Yemen mein Houthiyon ke kabze wale khetra ke saamne. Saudi netritva wala gathbandhan varshon se Yemen sarkar ko Houthiyon ke khilaf samarthan deta aaya hai, aur seema khetra shant samay mein bhi tanav mein rehte hain.

    Yeh hafta shant nahi tha. Pichhle dino Saudi hawai addon par Houthi hamlo ki report aayi, jismein gat hafte Riyadh ke King Khalid Antarastriya Hawai Adde par hue ghatak hamle mein kam se kam 13 log mare gaye the. Un hamlo ke baad, America ne apne sarkari karmachariyon ko vishesh anumati ke bagair Saudi hawai addon ka upyog karne se rok diya.

    Filhaal, pusht tathya kam hain. National Guard ka AH-6i Najran mein gira, adhikarik karan takneeki kharabi hai, aur do chalak dal ke sadasya mare gaye hain. Isse aage kuch bhi, yeh sahit ki Houthi dava usi viman ke baare mein hai ya nahi, kisi paksh dwara pusht nahi kiya gaya hai.

    Srot

    Gathbandhan ke bayan par Cairo se Reuters ki report, usi bayan par Arab News ki coverage, aur Sky News Arabia ki coverage.
