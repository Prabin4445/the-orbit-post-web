    Bitget $387.5 million hack ke hafto baad Thai exchange kharidega

    Bitget BTGT Management ke madhyam se Z.com EX ke peeche ke licensed Thai exchange ko kharidne ki yojana bana raha hai, hamlaawaro dwara uske wallet se lagbhag $387.5 million nikaale jaane ke hafto baad.

    OPलेखक Orbit Postअक्टूबर 20, 2026 · 16:45 UTC3 मिनट में पढ़ें
    शेयर करें
    इसमें पढ़ेंENESNEHIFR

    मुख्य बिंदु

    • Bitget ne mangalwar, October 20, 2026 ko kaha ki woh GMO-Z.com Cryptonomics ko kharidne ki yojana bana raha hai, Z.com EX platform ke peeche ka licensed Thai crypto exchange.
    • Sauda Bitget ko ek niyamit bazar mein tez marg deta hai.
    • Japan sthit GMO Internet ne pehle September 24, 2026 ko prastawit share hastantaran ka khulasa kiya.

    Bitget ne mangalwar, October 20, 2026 ko kaha ki woh GMO-Z.com Cryptonomics ko kharidne ki yojana bana raha hai, Z.com EX platform ke peeche ka licensed Thai crypto exchange. Khariddaar BTGT Management (Thailand) hai, ek company jisme Bitget ka hit hai jiska aakaar usne nahi bataya. Kharid keemat ka khulasa nahi kiya gaya.

    Sauda Bitget ko ek niyamit bazar mein tez marg deta hai. Z.com EX February 2021 mein Thai SEC aur Vitt Mantralaya ke licence ke saath launch hua. Licensed exchange kharidna ek naya banane aur shuru se avedan karne se tez hai, aur yahi is kharid ka mukhya tark hai.

    विज्ञापन

    Kharida gaya licence, banaya gaya nahi

    Japan sthit GMO Internet ne pehle September 24, 2026 ko prastawit share hastantaran ka khulasa kiya. Paksh Thai niyamak anumodan aur anya sharton ke adheen October 31, 2026 tak pura karne ka lakshya rakhte hain.

    Bitget CEO Gracy Chen ne kaha ki Thailand dakshin poorv Asia ke digital sampatti vikas ka mahatvapurna hissa hai aur sauda Bitget ko wahan sthaniya sanchalan sthapit karne ka mauka deta hai. Thailand mein sthaniya upasthiti mahatvapurna hai, jahan licence, sthaniya niyam aur niyamak bharosa tay karte hain ki kaun bade star par sanchalan kar sakta hai.

    Woh hack jisne kahani badal di

    Samay hi kahani hai. September 24, 2026 ko, usi din jab GMO ne saude ka khulasa kiya, Bitget ne hamlaawaro ko uske hot aur warm wallet khaali karte hue pakda. Nuksan anuman $351.6 million se badhkar lagbhag $387.5 million ho gaya.

    TRM Labs ne ise 2026 mein ekal crypto firm se sabse badi chori kaha. Bitget ne September 28, 2026 ko bitcoin nikaasi bahaal ki aur kaha ki grahak balance prabhawit nahi hue. Woh tathya nuksan ko nahi mitate. Woh frame karte hain ki niyamak aur grahak khariddaar ko kaise aakenge.

    Ek exchange jisne abhi record rakam khoyi hai, licence ke roop mein bharosa kharid raha hai. Bangkok ke niyamak tay karenge ki use woh milta hai ya nahi. Unki samiksha itne bade ullanghan ke baad licensed platform chalane ki suraksha, niyantran aur yogyata ka parikshan karegi.

    Thailand ek niyamit dwar kholta hai

    Thailand sandarbh samjhata hai ki Bitget ab kyun andar aana chahta hai. October 16, 2026 se laagu hue naye niyam yogya bitcoin aur ether ETF utpadon ko Stock Exchange of Thailand par soochibaddh hone dete hain, SEC har utpad ko anumodit karta hai.

    Woh badlav ek bazar ka sanket hai jo bina licence wale karobar se nahi, niyamit channelo se khul raha hai. Ek licensed sthaniya exchange us khulepan ke us videshi platform se zyada kareeb hai jiska koi Thai aadhar nahi hai.

    Aage kya hota hai

    Pura hona anumodan par nirbhar hai. Yadi Thai niyamak October 31, 2026 tak hastantaran anumodit karte hain, to Bitget ko licensed Thai sanchalan aur sthaniya team milegi. Yadi woh deri karte hain ya sakht sharte lagate hain, to samay sar jayega aur bharose ki laagat badhegi.

    Grahak hirasat, suraksha aur sthaniya nigrani par spasht bayaan dekhenge. Pratidwandi dekhenge ki hack ke baad ka adhigrahan dakshin poorv Asia mein pravesh ka model banta hai, ya chetavni banta hai ki licence bharose ke dobara banne se tez nahi kharide ja sakte.
