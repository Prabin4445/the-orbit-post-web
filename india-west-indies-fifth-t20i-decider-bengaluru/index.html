    India vs West Indies, 5th T20I: Bengaluru Decider, Time and Series Story So Far

    The series is level at 2-2. India and West Indies meet in the fifth T20I on Saturday at 7 p.m. IST at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 16, 2026 · 13:27 UTC · Updated October 17, 2026 17:35 UTC5 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • Updated 17:35 UTC.
    • Updated 12:45 UTC.
    • Updated 19:21 UTC.

    Updated 17:35 UTC. Rain washed out the decider in Bengaluru and the five match series ended level at 2-2. India made 118 for 5 in 7.4 overs in a game cut to nine overs a side before rain returned and the match was abandoned, IANS and CricketWorld reported. Ishan Kishan made 30 off 10 balls and Naman Dhir was unbeaten on 24. The match was called off at 10:16 p.m. IST because a five over chase of 84 for West Indies could not fit before the 10:21 p.m. cut off, IANS reported. The washout denied West Indies a first series win in India since 2002.

    Updated 12:45 UTC. Rain is the match-day story in Bengaluru. The India Meteorological Department has the city under a yellow alert for heavy rain and thunderstorms on Saturday, and both teams were unable to finish their practice sessions on the eve of the match because of rain. Temperatures are expected between 20 and 29 degrees, with a high chance of showers around the 6:30 p.m. IST toss. If the weather wipes the match out and fewer than five overs a side are possible, there is no result and no reserve day, and the series ends level at 2-2 with the trophy shared.

    Advertisement

    Updated 19:21 UTC. India have added veteran fast bowler Bhuvneshwar Kumar to the squad for the decider, the cricket board said on Friday, as Arshdeep Singh recovers from a blow to his bowling shoulder. Arshdeep was hit while batting in the fourth T20I in Hyderabad and retired hurt. A call on whether he plays in Bengaluru will be made before the match, Reuters reported. Bhuvneshwar, 36, returns to the India T20I setup for the first time in nearly four years, and he has also been named in the squad for the T20I series in New Zealand that starts on October 22, Cricbuzz reported.

    India and West Indies are level at 2-2, and the fifth and final T20I decides the series on Saturday, October 17 at 7 p.m. IST at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru. Four matches have produced four different swings. Neither side has held control for long, and the decider now comes down to one night at a ground known for high scores and short boundaries.

    West Indies forced the decider with a 24 run win in the fourth match in Hyderabad. They scored 247 for 3, and India replied with 223. Captain Shai Hope made an unbeaten 102 off 50 balls to set the total, and Keemo Paul took five wickets to close the chase. Those two performances turned a series India could have sealed into a final that both teams must win.

    The series so far

    India won the first match by eight wickets in Lucknow. West Indies answered in the second match in Ranchi, winning by six wickets. India moved ahead again in the third match in Indore, winning by 14 runs. West Indies then levelled the series in Hyderabad with that high scoring win.

    The pattern is clear. Wins have alternated, and no team has won two in a row. The side that bats with more calm in the first six overs and bowls better at the end will likely take the series. Bengaluru will test both parts of that plan.

    The Bengaluru factor

    Chinnaswamy Stadium is known for a batting friendly pitch and short boundaries. Average first innings scores sit around 180 or more. Batters can clear the ropes with clean hitting, and bowlers have little room for error in line or length.

    Dew is possible in the second innings. That can make the ball harder to grip and can help the chasing side. For that reason, the captain who wins the toss may choose to bowl first and chase a target with the dew in mind. The toss will draw close attention for that reason alone.

    Players to watch

    Abhishek Sharma has 436 runs at a strike rate of 225.90 across his last 10 matches, according to the preview figures. His fast starts give India early runs and force field changes. If he bats through the powerplay, India can aim well past 180.

    Shai Hope has 394 runs at a strike rate of 159.51 in the same span. His unbeaten hundred in Hyderabad showed his form and his control of a chase or a first innings build. West Indies will look to him again to anchor the innings and accelerate late.

    Keemo Paul also enters the decider with confidence after five wickets in the fourth match. On a ground that favors batters, a bowler who can change pace and hit the yorker late still has real value.

    Head to head and what it means

    India lead the overall T20I head to head 22-12, with one no result in 35 meetings. That record favors India on paper, but this series has not followed paper form. West Indies have matched India match for match and won the most recent game by a clear margin.

    Home support in Bengaluru will be loud for India. West Indies have shown they can handle that pressure and score fast on a good pitch. The decider should be close if both top orders fire.

    Key numbers

    • Series score: Level at 2-2 before the fifth T20I
    • Decider: Saturday, October 17 at 7 p.m. IST, M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru
    • Fourth match: West Indies 247 for 3, India 223, West Indies won by 24 runs
    • Shai Hope in match four: Unbeaten 102 off 50 balls
    • Keemo Paul in match four: Five wickets
    • Recent form: Abhishek Sharma 436 runs at 225.90 strike rate in last 10 matches
    • Recent form: Shai Hope 394 runs at 159.51 in last 10 matches
    • Overall T20I head to head: India 22 wins, West Indies 12, one no result

    How to watch and what happens next

    In India, the match is on the Star Sports Network and streaming on JioHotstar. Final lineups are confirmed only at the toss, so no predicted eleven should be treated as the team sheet. The winner takes the series 3-2. There is no next match to fix mistakes, and that simple fact should produce the best cricket of the series.
