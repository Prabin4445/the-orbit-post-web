    76ers Spoil Knicks Ring Night 106-100 as LeBron Debuts

    Tyrese Maxey scored 28 points as Philadelphia beat the defending champion Knicks at Madison Square Garden on the night New York raised its banner.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 21, 2026 · 2:35 UTC2 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • The Philadelphia 76ers spoiled a night of celebration at Madison Square Garden on Tuesday, beating the defending champion New York Knicks 106-100 in the NBA season opener on October 20, 2026.
    • Tyrese Maxey led all scorers with 28 points.
    • Philadelphia won the third quarter 29-18.

    The Philadelphia 76ers spoiled a night of celebration at Madison Square Garden on Tuesday, beating the defending champion New York Knicks 106-100 in the NBA season opener on October 20, 2026. Before tipoff, the Knicks raised their championship banner and received their rings, marking the club's first title in 53 years. By the final buzzer, the visitors had taken the floor, the lead and the story.

    Maxey sets the tone

    Tyrese Maxey led all scorers with 28 points. He added five assists, four rebounds, three steals and three blocks in a complete performance at both ends. Philadelphia needed every part of it. New York led 25-23 after the first quarter and 58-54 at halftime, with the second quarter going 33-31 to the Knicks. The game turned after the break.

    Advertisement

    Philadelphia won the third quarter 29-18. That swing gave the 76ers control, and they carried it into the fourth quarter even as New York took that period 24-23. The final margin, six points, matched how tight the night stayed from start to finish.

    New faces, familiar names

    LeBron James made his 76ers debut and opened a record 24th NBA season with 14 points, nine rebounds and seven assists. Jaylen Brown, also on debut for Philadelphia, scored 19 points with eight rebounds and six assists. Joel Embiid finished with 13 points, 11 rebounds and six assists.

    Anfernee Simons added 13 points and Kentavious Caldwell-Pope scored 10, both on debut, while VJ Edgecombe added nine. Philadelphia's new pieces did more than fill jerseys. They scored, they rebounded and they closed the game on the road.

    Brunson and the final seconds

    Jalen Brunson led New York with 21 points, five rebounds and three assists. The ending gave the home crowd one last surge of hope. Down four with 9.7 seconds left, Brunson missed a three point shot. He said he was fouled by Brown, then argued briefly with the former Celtics star. James grabbed the rebound, was fouled and made both free throws to seal the result.

    Why it matters

    This was more than an opening night win. Philadelphia rebuilt in the offseason, adding James and Brown, and the first test came in the home of the champions, on the night the champions celebrated. Winning there sends a clear signal about what the 76ers believe this group can become. For New York, the loss does not erase the banner or the rings. It does underline the harder task ahead.

    What happens next

    New York is trying to repeat as champion, something no team has done since Golden State in 2017 and 2018. Every contender will measure itself against the Knicks this season, and Philadelphia just set an early marker. The 76ers, meanwhile, have to show that a rebuilt roster can hold together over a full season. One night proves little on its own. But as statements go, winning 106-100 at Madison Square Garden on ring night is a strong place to start.
