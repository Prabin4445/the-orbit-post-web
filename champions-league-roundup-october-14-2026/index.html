    PSG Beat Man City 4-2, Real Madrid Stun Roma 4-3 in Champions League

    Paris Saint-Germain led 3-0 at half time at the Etihad. Real Madrid scored three goals in eight minutes to win 4-3 in Rome, Liverpool won 6-3 at LASK, and Aston Villa beat Fenerbahce 6-0.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 14, 2026 · 21:25 UTC · Updated October 15, 2026 20:16 UTC3 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • **Updated 20:16 UTC, October 15, 2026: Matchday 2 is complete and the full results are confirmed.
    • One detail can now be filled in from Rome.
    • Full Matchday 2 results.

    **Updated 20:16 UTC, October 15, 2026: Matchday 2 is complete and the full results are confirmed. The 18 games produced 78 goals, a Champions League record for a single matchday, beating the old mark of 71. Wednesday night alone brought 46 goals across the nine games below.**

    One detail can now be filled in from Rome. Madrid's three goals in eight minutes came from two substitutes and a defender: Carlos Espi scored in the 82nd minute, Endrick levelled in the 85th, and Antonio Rudiger scored the winner in the 90th.

    Advertisement

    Full Matchday 2 results. Tuesday, Oct. 13: Lens 0-2 Sporting CP, Sabah 2-0 Slavia Prague, Arsenal 3-0 Lille, Atletico Madrid 2-0 Manchester United, Galatasaray 1-4 Barcelona, Inter 3-4 Club Brugge, RB Leipzig 1-0 PSV Eindhoven, Viking 3-3 Bayern Munich, Villarreal 3-1 Napoli. Wednesday, Oct. 14: Feyenoord 3-2 Como, LASK 3-6 Liverpool, Roma 3-4 Real Madrid, Aston Villa 6-0 Fenerbahce, Shakhtar Donetsk 3-1 AEK Athens, Bodo/Glimt 1-0 Borussia Dortmund, Manchester City 2-4 PSG, Real Betis 3-2 Porto, Slovan Bratislava 0-3 Stuttgart. Tuesday's nine games were covered in our October 13 roundup.

    Seven teams have won both of their games: Paris Saint-Germain, Liverpool, Real Madrid, Aston Villa, Barcelona, Arsenal and Sporting CP. Bayern Munich are on four points after the draw at Viking. Manchester City stay on three points, all of them from the opening win at Porto, and have ground to make up after two rounds.

    Paris Saint-Germain beat Manchester City 4-2 at the Etihad Stadium on Wednesday night, Oct. 14, in the biggest result of Champions League Matchday 2, while Real Madrid produced the comeback of the night in Rome.

    PSG led 3-0 at half time. Desire Doue opened the scoring in the 13th minute, Ousmane Dembele headed the second minutes later, and Vitinha drove in the third from the edge of the box before the break. Maghnes Akliouche made it 4-1 in the second half. Erling Haaland scored twice for City after half time, taking his Champions League tally to 61 goals in 60 games, but the fightback fell short.

    Real Madrid turn 3-1 down into 4-3 in Rome

    Roma led through Marten de Roon, Kylian Mbappe levelled within minutes, and Donyell Malen scored twice to put Roma 3-1 up. Madrid then scored three goals in eight minutes late on. Antonio Rudiger got the winner as Jose Mourinho's side completed a 4-3 win at the Stadio Olimpico.

    Nine goals in Linz, six for Villa

    Liverpool won 6-3 at LASK Linz in the early kickoff. Bradley Barcola scored a hat trick, and Ronald Araujo, Jeremie Frimpong, and Lewis Koumas also scored for Liverpool. Florian Flecker scored twice for LASK and Robert Ljubicic added the third. Aston Villa beat Fenerbahce 6-0 at Villa Park, with doubles from Nicolas Jackson and Ross Barkley and a birthday goal for Yohan Manzambi.

    The rest of the night

    Bodo/Glimt beat Borussia Dortmund 1-0 in Norway. Real Betis beat Porto 3-2, Shakhtar Donetsk beat AEK Athens 3-1, Stuttgart won 3-0 at Slovan Bratislava, and Feyenoord beat Como 3-2 in Rotterdam, holding on after a stoppage time goal from Anastasios Douvikas.

    Full time scores, Oct. 14: LASK 3-6 Liverpool, Feyenoord 3-2 Como, Manchester City 2-4 PSG, Roma 3-4 Real Madrid, Aston Villa 6-0 Fenerbahce, Bodo/Glimt 1-0 Borussia Dortmund, Real Betis 3-2 Porto, Shakhtar Donetsk 3-1 AEK Athens, Slovan Bratislava 0-3 Stuttgart.

    What it means

    PSG, the holders, sit in control of their campaign after two rounds. Madrid's late surge in Rome is the kind of result that resets a season. For City, a home defeat by four goals conceded leaves no room for another slip in the league phase.
