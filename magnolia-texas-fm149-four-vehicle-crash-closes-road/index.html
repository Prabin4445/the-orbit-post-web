    Magnolia, Texas: Four-Vehicle Crash Closes FM 149 in Both Directions Near Magnolia

    Montgomery County deputies, fire crews and paramedics closed FM 149 on Friday morning after a four-vehicle crash near Magnolia. KPRC reported at least two people critically hurt in early reports and said the sheriff's office was treating the crash as a delayed fatal accident.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 23, 2026 · 20:56 UTC3 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • A four-vehicle crash closed FM 149 in both directions near Magnolia, in Montgomery County, on Friday morning, October 23.
    • KPRC described a road shut down in both directions, with multiple sheriff's office, fire and EMS crews on site.
    • FOX 26 Houston placed the closure at the intersection of Highway 149 and FM 1488, east of Magnolia, and said Highway 149 was closed in both the northbound and southbound directions.

    A four-vehicle crash closed FM 149 in both directions near Magnolia, in Montgomery County, on Friday morning, October 23. The Montgomery County Sheriff's Office said its units were arriving at FM 149 near Magnolia Hills, where deputies worked alongside fire and EMS crews, KPRC 2 reported. Drivers were told to stay away from the stretch while crews worked at the scene and investigators began their work.

    KPRC described a road shut down in both directions, with multiple sheriff's office, fire and EMS crews on site. Its county page carried the first detailed summary of the morning: a four-vehicle crash near Magnolia Hills, preliminary reports of at least two people critically injured, and the sheriff's office treating the incident as a delayed fatal accident. That wording signals how serious the crash was, and it also marks the limit of what officials had confirmed in the first reports. KPRC had published no names by Friday afternoon, and no official death count had been released in the reports available to this newsroom.

    Advertisement

    FOX 26 Houston placed the closure at the intersection of Highway 149 and FM 1488, east of Magnolia, and said Highway 149 was closed in both the northbound and southbound directions. The station called it a breaking story on Friday morning and said Montgomery County law enforcement was investigating the crash. It asked drivers to avoid the area. The two stations described the same Friday morning closure from different points along the same road, east of Magnolia and near Magnolia Hills.

    A local account from Montgomery County Police Reporter, carried in the county news roundup, put first responders at FM 149 and Karen Switch Road at 6:35 a.m. on Friday, at a four-vehicle crash with injuries. Its 7:38 a.m. update reported a 45-minute extrication and three patients in critical condition, with FM 149 closed along the crash stretch. That patient count is higher than the at least two critically injured people in KPRC's preliminary summary. Early counts from a crash scene often change as patients are moved and hospitals report back, and neither station had published a final injury total by Friday afternoon.

    What caused four vehicles to collide on FM 149 on Friday morning had not been released. Neither KPRC nor FOX 26 reported a cause, a vehicle description beyond the four-vehicle count, or a time for the road to reopen. FOX 26 said its article would be updated as more information was released, which fits a crash still under active investigation on Friday.

    For drivers in Magnolia and the west side of Montgomery County, the closure cut a direct local route in both directions during the Friday morning commute, with deputies asking traffic to take other roads. Anyone who drove FM 149 near Karen Switch Road, Magnolia Hills or FM 1488 around the time of the crash, and who saw the collision or the moments before it, holds information investigators usually ask for first in a multi-vehicle case. This story will be updated in place if the sheriff's office releases names, a final injury count or a cause.
