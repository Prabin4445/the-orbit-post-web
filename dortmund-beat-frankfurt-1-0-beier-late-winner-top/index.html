    Dortmund Beat Frankfurt 1-0 as Beier Scores Late Winner to Send Them Top

    Maximilian Beier scored the only goal after 80 minutes at Signal Iduna Park on Saturday, October 24, 2026, as Borussia Dortmund beat Eintracht Frankfurt 1-0 and moved to first place in the Bundesliga with 17 points.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 24, 2026 · 18:36 UTC2 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • Borussia Dortmund beat Eintracht Frankfurt 1-0 at Signal Iduna Park on Saturday, October 24, 2026, in Bundesliga Matchday 7.
    • Neither side could find a goal before the break.
    • Dortmund made a triple change in the 68th minute.

    Borussia Dortmund beat Eintracht Frankfurt 1-0 at Signal Iduna Park on Saturday, October 24, 2026, in Bundesliga Matchday 7. The first half finished without any goals, and the match stayed level deep into the second half. Maximilian Beier scored the only goal after 80 minutes, 12 minutes after coming on as a substitute. The win took Dortmund to first place in the Bundesliga table with 17 points.

    A goalless first half

    Neither side could find a goal before the break. Dortmund, coached by Niko Kovac, came into the match from two draws. Frankfurt, coached by Adi Hutter, came in from a win. The pattern of the first half matched two teams in different runs of form but close on the day: Dortmund pressed their home advantage without a finish, and Frankfurt held their shape and kept the home crowd waiting. The sides went in at 0-0, and the longer the match stayed level, the more the result turned on the changes made from the bench.

    Advertisement

    The triple change that decided the match

    Dortmund made a triple change in the 68th minute. Beier came on in place of Daniel Svensson, and the same move also brought on Ethan Nwaneri and Carney Chukwuemeka. The change gave Dortmund fresh legs for the final stretch of a match that had produced no goals in more than an hour. Beier needed 12 minutes to make the change count.

    Beier strikes from outside the box

    Beier scored after 80 minutes with a right foot strike from outside the penalty box. Jobe Bellingham, who had come on in the 79th minute, was recorded with the assist. The goal arrived one minute after Bellingham entered the match, which made the closing stage a story of the substitutes. Two players who started the evening on the bench combined for the goal that settled it, and Signal Iduna Park had its winner with 10 minutes left to play.

    Soccerzz described the finish as a close encounter, and the scoreline backs that reading. Frankfurt stayed in the match until the 80th minute and left with nothing, while Dortmund took all three points from a single moment of quality late in the game.

    Top of the table

    After the result, Dortmund sit first in the Bundesliga table with 17 points. Frankfurt sit 11th with 8 points. The gap between the two sides in the table tells the wider story of their starts to the season: Dortmund have turned a run that included two straight draws into first place, while Frankfurt remain in the lower half despite arriving in Dortmund on the back of a win.

    The next fixtures keep the pressure on both clubs. Dortmund next visit Bayern Munich, the hardest trip on the Bundesliga calendar and a direct test of their place at the top. Frankfurt next host Hamburger SV, a home match they will see as a chance to climb away from 11th place.
