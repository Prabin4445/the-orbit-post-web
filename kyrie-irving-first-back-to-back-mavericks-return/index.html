    Kyrie Irving Cleared for First Back-to-Back With Mavericks

    Kyrie Irving is not on the Dallas injury report for games at Indiana on Friday and against San Antonio on Saturday. It is the clearest sign yet in his return from a torn ACL that cost him all of last season.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 23, 2026 · 2:25 UTC3 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • Kyrie Irving is not on the Dallas Mavericks injury report for the team's first back-to-back of the season, a step that says more about his recovery than any workout video could.
    • Dallas plays at Indiana on Friday and then hosts San Antonio on Saturday in its home opener.
    • Irving returned on Wednesday in Houston.

    Kyrie Irving is not on the Dallas Mavericks injury report for the team's first back-to-back of the season, a step that says more about his recovery than any workout video could.

    Dallas plays at Indiana on Friday and then hosts San Antonio on Saturday in its home opener. Dallas Morning News reporter Mike Curtis flagged the point on Thursday, October 22. Irving's absence from the report means he is set to play on consecutive nights for the first time since his return, with the Pacers game first and the Spurs game factored in the next day.

    Advertisement

    Irving returned on Wednesday in Houston. He scored 15 points in 24 minutes, shooting 6 of 9 from the field, in a 128-110 loss to the Rockets. It was his first regular season game since March 3, 2025, when he tore the ACL in his knee against the Sacramento Kings. The injury ended that season for him and cost him the entire 2025-26 season.

    Four teammates are still out

    The rest of the Dallas report shows why Irving's name matters. Santi Aldama, after right knee surgery, Tarik Biberovic, with right heel bursitis, Dereck Lively II, after right foot surgery, and Caleb Martin, with a right thumb sprain, will all miss a second straight game, according to the team report noted by Christian Clark of The Athletic and summarized by Hoops Rumors on October 22. Two-way player Jett Howard, with right calf soreness, is doubtful.

    Dallas handled Irving with care before the season started. He was held out of an October 14 preseason game against New Orleans for rest. At the team's September media day, Irving said he was ready to play and was working without physical restrictions, the Associated Press reported at the time. His own words were direct. He said he was ready to go out there and compete hard, in the quote carried from that media day session.

    Why a back-to-back is the real test

    Load management is the normal path back from a torn ACL. Teams rest a returning guard on one night of a back-to-back as a matter of routine, often for months. Clearing a player for both nights in the first week of the season signals that the medical staff and the player both judge the knee ready for game load on short rest, not just for a single planned outing.

    It does not mean his minutes are free. Dallas can still cap him on Friday or Saturday, and one quiet night would not reverse the signal. The signal is the listing itself. He is treated as a healthy player on the report, in a week when four teammates are listed out with named injuries.

    Cooper Flagg scored 32 points in the Houston opener, a league record for a teenager in a season opener, and Dallas still lost by 18. The Mavericks finished 12th in the Western Conference last season at 26-56 while Irving sat out. His return is the piece that decides whether this roster is a playoff team or a young team learning on the job.

    What happens next

    Friday at Indiana is the first half of the test. The Pacers will be without backup point guard T.J. McConnell, who has a right hamstring strain and missed their opener on Wednesday. Saturday brings San Antonio to Dallas for the home opener. If Irving plays both games at close to his Houston minutes, the return moves from a comeback story to a normal season. If Dallas pulls him late from one of them, the report the next week will tell that story instead.
