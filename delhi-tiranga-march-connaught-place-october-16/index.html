    Delhi Locks Down Connaught Place for CJP Tiranga March as Central Park Stays Shut

    Police deployed thousands of personnel, closed Central Park for two days and restricted 20 roads after denying permission for the Cockroach Janta Party flag march set for 5 p.m. on Friday.

    OPBy Orbit PostOctober 16, 2026 · 13:24 UTC · Updated October 17, 2026 12:02 UTC6 min read
    Share
    Read inENESNEHIFR

    Key points

    • Updated 12:02 UTC.
    • Updated Oct 17, 05:06 UTC.
    • Updated 19:21 UTC.

    Updated 12:02 UTC. A Special Executive Magistrate court in Delhi granted conditional bail to Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke on Saturday, Bar and Bench and Latestly reported. Delhi Police said he will be released after signing a peace bond. The court imposed certain conditions, and full details of those conditions are still awaited, Bar and Bench reported. Dipke was produced before the court on Saturday after his preventive arrest on Friday ahead of the Lehra Do Tiranga march at Connaught Place.

    Updated Oct 17, 05:06 UTC. Delhi Police placed Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke under preventive arrest, converting his Friday detention into a formal arrest under Sections 126 and 170 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and said he would be produced before a Special Executive Magistrate court on Saturday, Reuters and LiveMint reported. Police said the arrest was to prevent a breach of the peace. Dipke had been granted conditional bail on Oct 10, 2026 on a condition that he keep the peace for one year, The Daily Jagran reported. AISA chief Neha Bora, who was detained at Mandi House on Friday, was released early on Saturday after formalities under Section 65 of the Delhi Police Act. The CJP said its lawyers had not yet been able to meet Dipke. Delhi Police did not immediately respond to a request for comment, Reuters reported.

    Updated 19:21 UTC. Delhi Police detained Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke in preventive detention on Friday as he left a house in Delhi, before he could reach Connaught Place for the Lehra Do Tiranga march. An officer told him the Section 163 orders were in force in Connaught Place, Reuters reported from live images shared by his group. Police also detained All India Students Association chief Neha Bora at Mandi House, and about 150 protesters were detained in total as hundreds of people carrying the national flag gathered at Connaught Place and Mandi House despite permission being denied, Latestly reported. Police called the detentions preventive. The CJP and the student group later said they did not know where Dipke and Bora had been taken. In Mumbai, police gave the CJP permission to hold a solidarity rally at Azad Maidan.

    New Delhi shut down one of its busiest shopping and office districts on Friday, Oct 16, 2026, as police prepared for a flag march called by the Cockroach Janta Party at Connaught Place. The party, known as the CJP, called the march Lehra Do Tiranga and set it for 5 p.m. at Central Park. Police denied permission for the march, closed ranks around Rajiv Chowk and nearby markets, and restricted traffic on 20 roads from 3 p.m.

    A heavy security cordon in the city centre

    More than 6,000 Delhi Police personnel took positions around Connaught Place on Friday, backed by 220 companies of the Central Armed Police Forces. Officers covered Rajiv Chowk, the Connaught Place markets, Central Park, Jantar Mantar, Palika Bazaar and nearby metro stations. About 5,000 more personnel stayed in reserve. Police said they expected a crowd of 30,000 to 50,000 people if supporters answered the call. Teams also installed more than 10 new CCTV cameras around Central Park to watch the gathering in real time.

    A prohibitory order under Section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita is in force in the area. The order lets police stop unlawful assemblies and act quickly if a crowd forms without permission.

    Central Park closed for two days

    The New Delhi Municipal Council closed Central Park to the public on Oct 16, 2026 and Oct 17, 2026, citing administrative reasons. The closure, announced on Oct 15, 2026, removed the exact ground where the march was set to gather. Central Park sits above Rajiv Chowk metro station and is one of the few large open spaces in the district, which is why protest calls often name it and why authorities move early to seal it.

    Police denied permission for the march and served a notice on CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke, warning against holding it without permission. Officers said a small select group might be allowed to carry the flag at another suitable location instead. Reports said AISA chief Neha Bohra was detained at Mandi House on her way to the march. In Mumbai, by contrast, police permitted a CJP flag demonstration at Azad Maidan, a ground long used for public meetings in that city.

    Traffic restricted on 20 roads

    A traffic advisory restricted or diverted traffic on 20 roads around Connaught Place from 3 p.m. on Friday. The list included Sansad Marg, Janpath, Barakhamba Road and Kasturba Gandhi Marg, all main routes used by government staff, shoppers and tourists. Commuters were told to expect delays through the evening peak and to use the metro where possible. For a district built around offices, hotels and markets, even a few hours of closure hits deliveries, shifts and evening trade.

    Why it matters

    The march is part of a wider campaign by the movement, which demands the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged irregularities in the electoral roll revision. That demand puts the protest at the centre of a national argument about voter lists and trust in elections. How police handle a march built around the national flag also draws close attention, because the flag carries strong public feeling and both sides know the images will travel fast online.

    The context

    Tension has built through the week. On Oct 10, 2026, a protest at Jantar Mantar drew a large security response, and roughly 26,000 CAPF personnel have been in the capital since that day. On Oct 13, 2026, CJP co-convenors Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka were arrested after entering Jantar Mantar with the national flag. Jantar Mantar, for years the best known protest site in the capital, has been denotified as a protest site, a change that has pushed new protest calls toward other central locations such as Connaught Place. On Oct 15, 2026, the Delhi High Court was told by police that legal measures would be taken to manage the protest.

    Key numbers

    Police on the ground: more than 6,000 Delhi Police personnel, plus about 5,000 in reserve. Central forces: 220 companies deployed for Friday, with roughly 26,000 CAPF personnel in the capital since Oct 10, 2026. Expected crowd: 30,000 to 50,000. New CCTV cameras around Central Park: more than 10. Roads restricted or diverted: 20, from 3 p.m. Park closure: Oct 16, 2026 and Oct 17, 2026.

    What happens next

    The test comes at 5 p.m., when the march was set to start. Police have offered a narrow alternative, a small select group carrying the flag at another suitable location, and the CJP must decide whether to accept it or test the ban in Connaught Place. Any detention or clash will be filmed and shared within minutes, so both sides face pressure to keep the evening calm. The park closure runs through Saturday, and traffic curbs may be extended if crowds stay in the area after dark.
