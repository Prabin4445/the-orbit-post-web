    Contact The Orbit Post

    One inbox, read by the editor. Pick the subject that fits and we will get back to you.

    News tips

    Seen something we should cover? Send tips, documents, photos, or video to hello@theorbitpostnews.us with "News tip" in the subject line. Tell us what happened, where, and when, and how you know. If you send photos or video, say whether you took them yourself. We do not pay for tips, and we will not name a source who asks to stay unnamed without talking it through with them first.

    Corrections

    We get things wrong sometimes. Tell us and we fix it. If you spot an error in a story, email hello@theorbitpostnews.us with "Correction" in the subject line and a link to the story. We check every correction against our sources. When a story changes in a way that matters, we fix the article and note the correction on the page itself, so readers can see what changed.

    Press and partnerships

    Media enquiries, syndication questions, and partnership proposals go to hello@theorbitpostnews.us with "Press" in the subject line. The Orbit Post is an independent publication. We do not sell news coverage, and we do not publish sponsored articles dressed up as reporting.

    Advertising

    Questions about the ads on this site, including an ad you believe is misleading or broken, go to hello@theorbitpostnews.us with "Advertising" in the subject line. Advertising never influences what we cover or how we cover it. To control how ads use cookies, see our Privacy Policy.

    Response times

    This is a small newsroom and the editor reads every message. Urgent corrections and news tips are handled first, usually the same day. Everything else gets an answer within two working days where an answer is needed. We cannot reply to messages that are abusive or that ask us to publish claims with no evidence behind them.

    The Orbit Post is an independent daily news site covering world, business, tech, sports and culture, original summaries with linked sources.

    Corrections: we publish corrections promptly. Tell us about any error.
