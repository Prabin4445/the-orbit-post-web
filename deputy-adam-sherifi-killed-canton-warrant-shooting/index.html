    Van Zandt County Deputy Adam Sherifi Killed in Shootout While Serving Warrants Near Canton

    A Van Zandt County sheriff's deputy was killed and a Canton police officer was wounded on Tuesday night when a man inside a house south of Canton, Texas, opened fire on officers serving felony warrants, the sheriff's office said. The suspect was found dead inside.

    Updated 12:28 UTC, Oct 24

    Sheriff Kevin Bridger said on Friday that the gunman who killed Deputy Adam Sherifi fired more than 800 rounds from a semiautomatic rifle before he was found dead inside the house south of Canton. Bridger identified the gunman as Mi Van Nguyen, 42, who was wanted on two felony warrants, for aggravated assault with a deadly weapon and for aggravated assault on a peace officer.

    "Think about that. 800 rounds shot at the men and women whose only purpose was to carry out their duties to protect this community," Bridger said at a news conference outside the sheriff's office. He gave no information about a possible motive and did not take questions from reporters.

    Advertisement

    Bridger said Deputy Brandon Fonteneau, who was with Sherifi and Canton Officer Chris Watson when the shooting began, gave lifesaving care to Watson, returned fire and helped other deputies and state personnel reach the wounded officer. Watson remains in hospital and is recovering, the sheriff said. Funeral services for Sherifi are scheduled for Wednesday, Oct. 28, at 1 p.m. in Canton, according to the sheriff's office. The Texas Rangers are investigating.

    A Van Zandt County sheriff's deputy has been killed and a Canton police officer wounded in a shootout while officers served felony warrants at a house south of Canton, Texas, on Tuesday night, the sheriff's office said.

    Deputies were helping Canton police serve warrants at a home in the 4300 block of State Highway 198, according to the Van Zandt County Sheriff's Office, in a statement reported by The Dallas Morning News, FOX 4 and KLTV. The suspect had outstanding felony warrants for aggravated assault and aggravated assault on a peace officer.

    At about 10:22 p.m. on Tuesday, Oct. 20, officers reported shots fired from inside the house toward the deputies and officers outside. Officers returned fire, the sheriff's office said.

    Deputy named, officer flown to hospital

    The deputy who was killed was identified on Wednesday as Adam Sherifi. His brother identified him, CBS19 reported, and Van Zandt County Court at Law Judge Joshua Wintters confirmed his name to KLTV. Sherifi leaves a wife and three children, one of them born in September, Police1 and CBS19 reported.

    The wounded Canton officer was identified as Christopher Watson. He was shot in the arm, flown to a hospital in Plano for surgery, and faced a possible amputation, according to reports citing county officials. By midday Wednesday the wounded officer was in stable condition, FOX 4 reported.

    Officers found the suspect dead inside the house from a gunshot wound. Investigators have not said whether the wound was self inflicted or came from officers returning fire. The sheriff's office described the shooting as an isolated incident with no ongoing threat to the public.

    Rangers investigate, county gathers

    The Texas Rangers, part of the Texas Department of Public Safety, are investigating the shooting.

    Community members gathered at noon on Wednesday on the front steps of the Van Zandt County Courthouse to pray for Sherifi's family and for the wounded officer, FOX 4 reported. The City of Canton asked residents to pray for the deputy's family and for the injured officer. State Sen. Bob Hall, who represents Van Zandt County, and U.S. Rep. Lance Gooden also asked people to pray for both families, according to reports.

    "My heart goes out to the Van Zandt County Sheriff's Office as they mourn the loss of Deputy Adam Sherifi," Malakoff Mayor Nicole Mason-Driver wrote, in a post reported by WFAA. "I extend my deepest condolences to his wife, his children, his loved ones, and his entire law enforcement family."

    Hours after the shooting, the same county was hit by a second tragedy, when a house fire near Canton killed a mother and her four young children early on Wednesday. County officials dealt with both losses on the same day.
